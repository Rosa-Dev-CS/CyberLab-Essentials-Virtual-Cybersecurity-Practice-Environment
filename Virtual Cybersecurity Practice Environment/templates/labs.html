Practical Training Exercises

Apply your knowledge in your local hypervisor. Review tasks below, perform commands in your virtual lab, answer the verification checks, and auto-draft lab reports directly into your journal.

Objective:

Boot your Kali Linux VM and Metasploitable VM. Perform an active ping sweep on your Host-Only subnet using Nmap to identify the IP address of your Metasploitable target.

$ nmap -sn 192.168.56.0/24

Objective:

Run a detailed service scan against the Metasploitable VM to enumerate running services, server software versions, and open system daemon sockets.

$ nmap -sS -sV 192.168.56.103

Objective:

Launch Wireshark on Kali Linux, listen on the Host-Only interface (eth1), and perform a simple telnet login connection to Metasploitable. Stop the capture and analyze the raw telnet stream contents to see how cleartext protocols leak data.

$ telnet 192.168.56.103

Objective:

Metasploitable hosts several web utilities. Open a browser in Kali, navigate to http://192.168.56.103/mutillidae or http://192.168.56.103/dvwa and explore their structures defensively.

Objective:

Inspect the target's configuration files to audit weak permissions. Find files writable by standard users or set to world-write permissions (`chmod 777`).
